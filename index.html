Random Loading

Containers loaded onto a vessel other than their designated vessel — full traceability & accountability
🎲 Random loading happens when a container is loaded onto a vessel/voyage different from what it was designated for — due to vessel planning, yard management, shutout replacement, or other operational reasons. These are captured automatically wherever containers are loaded (Vessel Loading, Quayside direct load, Yard direct-to-vessel), and are fully searchable and reportable here.
